Hands-on introduction to modeling content in Enonic and reading it through the Guillotine GraphQL API.

== Introduction

This tutorial is for developers who define content types and build front-ends against Enonic's GraphQL API. It covers content modeling, schema management and the Guillotine API, using the Enonic CLI, Content Studio and a local sandbox. Every chapter builds on the previous one, so you gain understanding incrementally while building a small content model of your own.

Along the way you will:

* <<setup#, set up>> the CLI, a local sandbox and your own app
* create content types and content in <<content-studio#, Content Studio>>
* query your content through the <<headless-api#, Headless API>>
* upload <<media#, images and other media>>, and serve them scaled and cropped
* model richer content with <<form-items#, form items>>, <<sets#, sets and form fragments>>, <<rich-text#, rich text>> and <<mixins#, mixins>>
* publish your content and explore <<permissions#, read and editorial permissions>>
* <<export-import#, export and import>> your content into another project

Two things to know before you start:

* *No coding.* You will not write application code. You will edit YAML schemas, run GraphQL queries, and use ready-to-run cURL requests to test the API. There is nothing to compile or debug.
* *No pages.* Sites, pages, layouts and parts - the whole page domain of Enonic - are left out entirely. To learn more about pages, check out the https://developer.enonic.com/docs/next.xp[Next.js tutorial], the https://developer.enonic.com/learn/react4xp-tutorial/stable[React4XP tutorial], or the https://developer.enonic.com/learn/building-enonic-sites/stable[My First Site] tutorials.

The https://developer.enonic.com/docs/cms[CMS reference documentation] covers every topic in this tutorial in depth, and is the place to go once you know what you are looking for.

NOTE: When we mention "XP", we are referring to "Enonic XP", the platform behind Enonic.

If you have any questions, or just want to get in touch, visit the https://discuss.enonic.com[Forum] or join the https://slack.enonic.com/[Community Slack channel].

The first step is to <<setup#, get your tools in place>>. Good luck! 🙌
