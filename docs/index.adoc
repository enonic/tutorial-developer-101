Hands-on introduction to modelling content in Enonic and reading it through the Guillotine GraphQL API.

== Introduction

This tutorial is for developers who define content types and build front-ends against Enonic's GraphQL API. It covers content modelling, schema management and the Guillotine API, using the Enonic CLI, Content Studio and a local sandbox. Every chapter builds on the previous one, so you gain understanding incrementally while building a small content model of your own.

Along the way you will:

* <<setup#, set up>> the CLI, a local sandbox and your own app
* create content types and content in <<content-studio#, Content Studio>>
* query your content through the <<headless-api#, Headless API>>
* upload <<files#, images and other media>>, and serve them scaled and cropped
* model richer content with <<form-items#, form items>>, <<sets#, sets and form fragments>>, <<rich-text#, rich text>> and <<mixins#, mixins>>
* fetch it all from a <<frontend#, front-end>>, with routing, paging and URLs
* look <<data-toolbox#, under the hood>> at how it is all stored

Two things to know before you start:

* *No coding.* You will not write application code. The schemas are YAML files, the queries are GraphQL, and the only JavaScript is a handful of `fetch` examples in the front-end chapter, which you can run as they are. There is nothing to compile or debug.
* *No pages.* Sites, pages, layouts and parts - the whole page domain of Enonic - are left out entirely, whether rendered by Enonic or by a front-end of your own. This tutorial is about the content itself and how you get it out through the API. For pages, see the https://developer.enonic.com/docs/intro[Introduction to Enonic] and the https://developer.enonic.com/docs/next.xp[Next.js tutorial].

The https://developer.enonic.com/docs/cms[CMS reference documentation] covers every topic in this tutorial in depth, and is the place to go once you know what you are looking for.

NOTE: When we mention "XP", we are referring to "Enonic XP", the platform behind Enonic.

If you have any questions, or just want to get in touch, visit the https://discuss.enonic.com[Forum] or join the https://slack.enonic.com/[Community Slack channel].

The first step is to <<setup#, get your tools in place>>. Good luck! 🙌
